Privacy Policy

This explains what Clean Boy Logbook keeps about you and where. The short version: your logbook stays on your phone; if you make an account, only your sign-in details are kept on a server.

What the app keeps

Everything you enter: your daily logs, notes, goals, lists, photos you add, and your settings. These stay on this phone. If you make an account we also keep: your email address, your password (held by our sign-in service; this app never stores it), a user ID, and sign-in records (when you signed in and with which method). If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive only what they pass on, usually your email address. If you turn on sync (optional, in Settings > Account), a copy of your whole logbook (entries, notes, photos and settings) is also stored in your account on our servers.

Where it is kept

Your logbook entries: only on this phone, in the browser's own storage for this app. They are never sent to us or anyone else, and are not encrypted inside the browser storage, so use a screen lock. Your account details: on the servers of our sign-in service (Supabase). Your sign-in is also remembered in the browser storage until you sign out. With sync on, the copy of your logbook is stored with our database service (Supabase), in a record only your signed-in account can read. It is protected in transit and at rest by the service, but it is not end-to-end encrypted: people who run the service could technically access it, and we do not read it. With sync off, nothing about your logbook leaves the phone.

What we do NOT do

No ads. No tracking or analytics. No selling or sharing of your logbook. The app only contacts the internet when you sign in, manage your account, buy or restore a purchase, or (if you turned it on) sync; a security setting on the page allows only those addresses.

Email updates

If you tick the optional box when you make an account, we may email you news and tips about our apps. That is the only reason we use your email for messages beyond the ones needed to run your account (such as confirming your email or resetting a password). You can say no, and you can unsubscribe at any time using the link in every update or by writing to hello@cleanroutineco.app. We do not sell or share your email address.

Your choices and control

You hold your logbook, so you control it. Settings > Your data: download a backup, restore one, or erase all entries. Settings > Account: sign out, or delete your account. Deleting the account removes your sign-in details from our sign-in service. It does not remove the logbook from the phone ("Delete everything on this phone" does that), and the billing and payment companies may keep purchase records as the law requires. Sync can be turned off, and the cloud copy deleted, at any time in Settings > Account.

How long it is kept

Logbook entries: for as long as you keep them on your phone. Account details: until you delete the account. We have no copy of your logbook and cannot restore it. The cloud copy is kept until you turn sync off and choose "Delete cloud copy", or delete your account, which removes it too.

Keeping it safe

Your password is handled by our sign-in service and is never stored in this app or in your logbook. Use a password you do not use anywhere else. Account data is protected by our sign-in service. Your logbook is protected only by your phone: use a screen lock. The entries are not encrypted inside the browser storage, so they cannot be protected from a hacked phone or someone with full control of your browser.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see, correct, delete or take a copy of the personal data held about you, and to complain to your data-protection authority. Your logbook is on your phone, so you already hold it: use Settings > Your data to download or erase it. For your account (your email and sign-in records), use Settings > Account > Delete account, which removes them from our sign-in service, or write to us at hello@cleanroutineco.app and we will help. Payment companies and app stores may keep purchase records for as long as tax and accounting law requires.

Children

The app is not meant for people under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from them. If you think a younger person has an account, email us and we will delete the account and its cloud copy. Logbook entries are not sent to us unless you turn on sync; account details are collected only from people who sign in.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the app asks you to read and agree again. Questions or requests: hello@cleanroutineco.app.

Cookie Policy

This explains what Clean Boy Logbook stores on your device and why. The short version: nothing for tracking.

The short version

Clean Boy Logbook does not use advertising cookies, tracking cookies or analytics cookies. It does not set cookies of its own to follow you around.

What it stores on your device

The app keeps its data in your browser's own storage for this app (local storage), which is similar to a cookie but is not sent to a server with every request. It holds: your logbook entries and settings; your light or dark choice; your sign-in on this phone (if you made an account); and a small record of your plan so the app can show it offline. This storage is needed for the app to work, so it is "strictly necessary" and does not need a cookie banner.

Sign-in and billing services

If you make an account, our sign-in service (Supabase) keeps your sign-in session in the same browser storage so you stay signed in. The billing service (Qonversion) may keep a random identifier there to remember your purchase. Neither is used for advertising.

Third-party pages

When you buy, the payment company's page (for example Stripe or Paddle) or the App Store opens. They may set their own cookies on their own pages to process the payment and prevent fraud. Those are covered by their own cookie and privacy policies, and we do not control them.

Your choices

You can clear this storage at any time: Settings > Account > "Delete everything on this phone", or your browser's "Clear site data". That also removes the logbook from this phone, so download a backup first (Settings > Your data). You can also block cookies in your browser settings; the app does not need any.
